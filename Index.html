ALLIPP Weekly Report

About this app

This is a personal automation tool that generates a weekly report on my own pension portfolio. It reads data from my own Google Sheets and Google Drive account and writes a summary PDF back to my Drive.

Privacy Policy

This app is used solely by its owner and accesses no data belonging to any other person. It does not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties, with one exception: the contents of the relevant spreadsheet data are sent to Anthropic's Claude API in order to generate the written narrative portion of the report. No other third-party sharing occurs.
